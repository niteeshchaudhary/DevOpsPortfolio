# TIMELINE.md

> Project: DevOps Portfolio
> Version: 1.0
> Purpose:
>
> This document defines the complete cinematic timeline of the portfolio.
>
> Every scroll advances the story.
>
> The visitor should feel like they are walking through a gigantic industrial
> facility that gradually comes alive.
>
> This timeline controls:
>
> - Camera movement
> - Asset animations
> - Project reveals
> - Environmental storytelling
> - Infrastructure state
> - Background changes
> - Sound triggers
> - Interactive moments

---

Birth of the Pipeline

ACT 2

Automation

ACT 3

Deployment
